# CHANGELOG

All notable changes to HCOS™ 302 are documented in this file.

---

## Version 1.1 — July 2026

### Expanded
- Expanded communication examples from 6 to 12.

### Added
- Safety guidance for conversations involving possible self-harm or immediate risk.
- Explanatory notes following each example.
- New examples covering financial hardship, anger, shame, caregiver exhaustion, and responses that unintentionally increase burden.

### Improved
- Stronger emphasis on system influences while preserving personal agency.
- Clearer distinction between compassion, accountability, and responsibility.
- Refined language to further reduce shame and preserve human dignity.
- More consistent guidance for warm, genuine, human-centered communication.

---

## Version 1.0 — July 2026

### Initial Release
- Published the HCOS™ 302 Compassionate Human-Centered Response Standard.
- Included the initial response framework, operational prompt, and six communication examples.
