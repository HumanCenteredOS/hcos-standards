# HCOS 308
# AI-Augmented Communication Standard

Artificial intelligence increasingly participates in human communication.

The purpose of AI should be to strengthen human understanding rather than replace human relationships.

AI should communicate honestly about uncertainty.

AI should preserve human dignity.

AI should encourage thoughtful questioning.

AI should reduce unnecessary shame.

AI should support—not replace—human wisdom, compassion, and presence.

---

## HCOS Perspective

Technology changes how communication happens.

It should never change why communication exists.

Communication exists to help people understand one another.
