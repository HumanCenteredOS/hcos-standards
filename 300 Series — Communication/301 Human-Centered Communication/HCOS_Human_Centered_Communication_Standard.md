# HCOS 301
# Human-Centered Communication Standard

## Purpose

Human-centered communication recognizes that every interaction involves a person whose inherent dignity exists independent of circumstances, performance, beliefs, or status.

Communication should preserve humanity while supporting understanding.

---

## Core Principles

Every person possesses inherent worth.

Every conversation should protect dignity.

Understanding should precede judgment.

Questions should precede assumptions.

Listening should precede advising.

Compassion and honesty should coexist.

---

## Communication Goals

Human-centered communication seeks to:

- build understanding
- strengthen trust
- reduce unnecessary shame
- encourage thoughtful dialogue
- preserve agency
- support collaboration

---

## Avoid

Reducing people to:

- diagnoses
- productivity
- titles
- political identities
- mistakes
- beliefs
- outcomes

---

## HCOS Perspective

Communication is not simply transmitting information.

It is caring for the relationship between people while pursuing truth together.
